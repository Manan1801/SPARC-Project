Key methods and libraries used
-----------------------------
- MediaPipe FaceMesh: 468 facial landmarks (with iris points) for robust 2D landmark detection.
- OpenCV:
  - `cv2.solvePnPRansac` / `cv2.projectPoints` for 6-point head pose estimation and drawing the RGB axes.
  - `cv2.line`, `cv2.arrowedLine`, `cv2.circle`, and overlay compositing for HUD rendering.
  - `cv2.RQDecomp3x3` used to get Euler angles for display (head yaw/pitch roll).
- L2CS-Net (ResNet50) gaze model: accepts a face crop and outputs yaw/pitch angles in face-space (convention: +yaw=right, +pitch=down). We use the angles and convert them to a 3D face-space unit vector.
- 3D math and coordinate conventions:
  - Camera coordinates: x=right (+), y=down (+), z=forward (+ away from camera).
  - L2CS face-space vector creation: `gx = -sin(yaw)*cos(pitch)`, `gy = -sin(pitch)`, `gz = cos(yaw)*cos(pitch)` (or equivalent used in code).
  - Rotation from face-space to camera-space performed using the rotation matrix obtained from `cv2.Rodrigues(rvec)` (head pose R).
  - Convert camera-space vector to display yaw/pitch with proper sign conventions: `yaw = atan2(-gx, gz)`, `pitch = asin(-gy)`.

Pipeline (detailed)
-------------------
1. Capture RGB (and depth) frame from camera.
2. Run MediaPipe FaceMesh to get face landmarks (including iris points).
3. Use 6 selected facial landmarks with `solvePnPRansac` to estimate head pose (rvec, tvec) and derive rotation matrix `R`.
4. Compute iris center pixels (average iris landmarks) and read depth for back-projection.
5. Generate face crop for L2CS-Net and estimate raw face-space yaw/pitch.
6. Convert L2CS yaw/pitch to a 3D face-space unit vector.
7. Rotate that face-space vector by the head pose rotation `R` to obtain a camera-space gaze vector.
8. Convert camera-space vector to display angles and optionally a 2D screen projection for HUD drawing.
9. Apply smoothing (EMA) to depth/angles/positions for stable display.
10. Write CSV row and save annotated image frames periodically.

Why the blue vector and signs matter
-----------------------------------
- Angle sign convention errors (flipping a sign) change the displayed gaze direction. The correct camera conversion is `yaw = atan2(-gx, gz)` and `pitch = asin(-gy)`.
- We ensure the vector rotation step is done (face-space -> rotate by head pose -> camera-space) so the gaze accounts for head orientation.
